Background

Russia covers the largest section of forest in the entire world. The Siberian wilderness is both as mysterious as it is untended. Due to lack of infrastructure such as railroads or traffic as well as extreme temperatures and geography it remains the largest untamed wilderness in the world untouched by civilization.

“Untamed wilderness” is an area that is free of human control, habitation or cultivation. Untamed wilderness is also sparsely documented, this means that what occurs there goes largely unnoticed by the public; this includes both natural and unnatural occurrences such as the interference from ungoverned human activity.

Issue

The effect of climate change is observed in many parts of the world, but how does it impact areas that aren’t observed? Climate change is far more widespread than what we are presently aware of. One of its impacts are on the untamed wilderness of the world

The Siberian taiga suffers from seasonal fires, but it has gotten to an extreme in recent years. The forest was on fire for most of 2019 burning 130,000 square km, experienced “mystery fires” from 2022 until present, and suffered a massive wildfire emergency in 2025 burning 130,000 more square km over 117 fires.

90% of the fires in Buryatia fires of 2025 were caused by human activity, further increasing the responsibility on humans to be mindful of their impact on the environment.

Local indigenous people also reported that deer populations appear “sickly” and unhealthy, making them in danger of not repopulating. The local habitats are being destroyed, and this further endangers the people who have historically taken care of the woods and their way of life.

Government Response

During the 2025 fires “Buryatia’s branch of the Federal Forestry Agency banned public access to the region’s forests.” They also banned grass burning ceremonies and cooking over open flame. The situation is desperate enough to put a ban on all human activity in the area. Despite that, the Siberian wilderness is too large to manage and it’s impossible to regulate all human activity. The great untamed wilderness has always been a hotbed for criminal activity such as smuggling and poaching. As a result, the forests are constantly at risk of human interference.

What Can You Do

We ask you to simply be aware of the impact humanity has on the environment and internalize how deep the rabbit hole goes in terms of the climate crisis in today’s world. We also ask you to be mindful of places under federal and regional preservation. Due to climate change, nature preserves have become much more sensitive to minute changes and remain fragile until conditions improve. Thank you for your time.
